✅ How to spot phishing next time

  1. Check the sender - the display name can lie; look at the real email address and domain.
  2. Hover before you click - preview the real URL. On mobile, long-press the link.
  3. Pause on urgency - "expires in 24 hours" and "account suspended" are designed to rush you.
  4. Verify out of band - open the app or site yourself instead of clicking an email link.
  5. Report it - use the Report Phishing button in your email client so IT can warn others.
